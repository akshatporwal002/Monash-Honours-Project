## Question

What evidence will satisfy the selected outcome, at its approved Bloom target?

- Define mandatory criteria, the pass rule, and evidence-sufficiency rules.
- Provide valid and invalid examples, including unusual valid answers and missing or conflicting evidence.
- State which criteria support reliable automatic checks and which require human judgement.
- Specify the approval pattern for later outcomes without treating one approved outcome as blanket approval.

A resolution must provide: Versioned assessor-approved criteria, rule, evidence requirements, examples, and evaluator boundaries for the selected outcome.
