# LearnLens

LearnLens supports introductory quantum learning, grounded teaching, and formal assessment.
These terms keep observations, estimates, and decisions distinct.

## Language

**Complete quantum learning loop**
A learning episode covering prediction, reasoning, circuit work, feedback, revision, reflection, and a fresh transfer activity.
Preserved evidence informs an updated learner estimate and the next approved activity.

**Learning evidence**
A preserved observation of a learner's work or learning conditions.
It is the basis for an estimate or assessment, rather than the estimate or result itself.

**Learner estimate**
A versioned inference about learning, linked to evidence and uncertainty.
It may guide teaching but cannot determine a formal result.

**Formal assessment result**
A PASS or INCOMPLETE result based on approved criteria and evidence.
A system result remains provisional until an authorised assessor acts; lifecycle state is separate from the result.

**Legacy assessment history**
A protected archive of original assessment values and their migration record.
It preserves past records without supplying a current formal result.
