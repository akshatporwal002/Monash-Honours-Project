# Task 39 tester handoff

Prepared against `27a397a66b5fb6544ba08d9c8950fbbc8c6b4ca4`. This is an executable
validation kit, not completed validation. Human result cells are intentionally blank.

1. Facilitator: follow [setup](setup.md), allocate a private run and fresh fixtures,
   and copy the [blank records](result-templates.md) to your approved evidence store.
2. Technical testers: execute the [role matrix](role-matrix.md), applying every
   [accessibility procedure](accessibility.md) to each listed state. Repeat the key
   processes in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and [native Safari](native-browsers.md).
3. First-time participants: receive only their [neutral trial card](usability.md).
   Keep the facilitator steps, expected answers and technical matrix out of view.
4. Record actual observations and exact versions. Attach defects with reproduction,
   expected/actual behaviour and evidence. Send the completed packet to the coordinator
   through the team's approved channel; this kit does not send or publish reports.

Use synthetic application accounts and content only. Human trial records are still
participant evidence: use pseudonymous participant IDs, agreed recording permissions,
and the applicable approved storage/retention process. Do not enable application
research exports to collect usability results. If research recruitment approval is
required and absent, prepare the session but leave that study trial unperformed.

The fixed acceptance targets are NFR1 average rating **at least 7/10**, NFR2 **at
least five first-time educator trials, each within 20 minutes**, NFR3 **at least
80% of first-time students completing sign-in → task → submission → feedback
unaided within 15 minutes**, NFR4 WCAG 2.2 AA key paths with no open critical access
fault, and NFR18 current native browser coverage. Sampling and scoring details in
the trial script are proposals awaiting the review owner's decision.

Pending decisions: named facilitator/accessibility reviewer, participant sampling,
approved instruments and recording/retention, exact trial source/task, Mac and
assistive technology availability, release build/environment, and defect owners.
Existing Task 8 choices are settled. Missing institutional, content-expert and AI
evaluator approval is not supplied by synthetic fixtures or this kit.

PASS/INCOMPLETE are formal assessment results, released with human confirmation.
Test statuses `PASS / FAIL / NOT RUN` below describe validation cases only. Hints
have no instructional count cap during supported work; unaided transfer is separate
and retains approved access support. Keep histories, uncertain estimates, operational
evidence, research measures and formal results distinct.

See [known dependencies](dependencies.md) before triage and the
[delivery record](../task-39-manual-validation-kit.md) for this preparation's checks.
