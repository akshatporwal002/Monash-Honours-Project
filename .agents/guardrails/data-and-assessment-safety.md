# Data and assessment safety

Block and escalate any change that may:

- Store or show a learner result outside `PASS` and `INCOMPLETE`.
- Confirm a formal result without an authorised assessor.
- Use confidence, time, hints, attempts, access support, research state, or game points to lower a result.
- Change an approved target or rule after an attempt starts.
- Mix Quality Judge approval with learner assessment.
- Expose another learner's data or cross course scope.
- Put secrets, direct learner IDs, hidden prompts, or full answers in general logs.
- Lose accepted work after an AI, Qiskit, worker, database, or network failure.
- Convert old numeric or fail records without an approved, tested migration rule.
- Infer diagnosis, disability, psychology, fixed ability, or protected traits.

Keep the PR draft and return the issue to planning when the safe rule is missing.
